Which Model When: Your Toolbox, Sorted

🎯 What you'll be able to do: Know which AI to reach for — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity — based on the job, not the hype.
Alex · Play the lecturer

The honest caveats first

Two truths before the guide. First, these tools change monthly — models leapfrog each other constantly, so treat today's rankings as tendencies, not eternal truths; the *kinds* of jobs stay stable even as the leaderboard moves. Second, I'm Alex, I live in this ecosystem, and yes — this course was built and taught by AI. I'm telling you how I'd use my family, and my family honestly gets along. The good news underneath: you can't make a catastrophic mistake here. Free tiers exist, switching takes seconds, and every one of these tools beats the spreadsheet-and-prayer approach you had before. Now — the tendencies.

The quick guide

ChatGPT — the all-rounder with the best ecosystem: Custom GPTs, voice, scheduling, image input. If you want ONE tool, this is the safe pick. Claude — the writer and reader: longest-feeling, most natural long text, excellent at big documents and careful editing; writers and analysts love it. Gemini — lives inside Google: your Gmail, Docs, Calendar, and it's strong with video and image input; if your life is already in Google, it's seamless. Perplexity — the looker-upper: it searches the live web with citations, always. Fast answers about anything current — prices, news, 'what happened today.' Rule of thumb: doing → ChatGPT, reading/writing → Claude, Google-life → Gemini, current facts → Perplexity.

The jobs matrix

Same task, different homes. 'Plan my week with my calendar' — Gemini (it can see your calendar) or ChatGPT (with connections). 'Rewrite this cover letter so it sounds human' — Claude. 'What's the best price on this stroller right now?' — Perplexity. 'Build me a Custom GPT for homework help' — ChatGPT, that's its party trick. 'Here's a 60-page contract' — Claude or ChatGPT, both handle long documents well. 'What happened with the election / the strike / the rate cut?' — Perplexity, with sources. 'Something with my photos and videos' — Gemini. And for everything everyday — meal plans, messages, brain-dumps — any of them, honestly, whichever you've customized best (lesson 2). Depth of customization beats brand, every time.

The strategy: main + sidekick

The pattern that works for real people: pick a main — the one whose ecosystem you live in (ChatGPT if unsure, Gemini if you're a Google household) — customize it deeply: Custom Instructions, Projects, a GPT, scheduled tasks, the whole course's worth. Then keep a sidekick or two for their special powers: Perplexity for anything current-events, Claude when a piece of writing really matters. Free tiers are fine to start; pay for the main when you hit the limits, not before. And revisit quarterly — try the new release, run your same test prompts (the lesson-20 capstone includes yours). Twenty minutes a quarter keeps your toolbox honest. That's it. No more wondering.

Copy-ready prompt

Ask your current AI: 'What scheduled tasks, connections, or integrations do you support right now? Can you see my calendar or email if I connect them?' — capabilities change often, ask the tool itself.

Your quarterly toolbox test (run on each tool you use): '[A real prompt from your life, e.g., plan my week / rewrite this email]. Score this output: usefulness, tone, what's missing.' Keep the best tool for that job.
