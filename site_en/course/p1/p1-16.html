Custom GPTs: Build Your Own Assistant

🎯 What you'll be able to do: Package everything you've learned — instructions, files, prompts — into your own named assistant you can share and reuse.
Alex · Play the lecturer

What a Custom GPT is

A Custom GPT (ChatGPT's term; Gemini has Gems, Claude has similar flavors) is a saved bundle: instructions + files + starter prompts + a name. Think of it as an employee file — role, training materials, job description — that you write once. Open the builder, describe what you want in plain English, and the platform drafts it; you refine. The magic moment is the first sentence of its instructions — that's the role. Everything else hangs off it. And unlike a normal chat, it's *there* tomorrow: same name, same brain, no rebuilding. You'll make one for each recurring job in your life.

Building your first one

Walkthrough: create the GPT, name it honestly — 'Family CFO,' 'Homework Buddy,' 'The Ad Writer' — and write the instructions like a job posting: 'You help a busy family manage money. Tone: direct, zero judgment. When given transactions, categorize and flag leaks (lesson 10 rules). When asked about big decisions, remind me to sleep on it. Never recommend products without a reason.' Then upload the files that make it smart (budget history, the voice profile from lesson 14) and add 3-4 starter prompts so future-you just clicks. Test it in the preview with a real task, refine, save. Fifteen minutes, first hire complete.

The ones people actually keep

The GPTs that survive real life share one trait: a narrow job. 'Family CFO' beats 'Assistant.' 'Homework Buddy — never gives answers, Socratic mode, patient with a 9-year-old' (lesson 11) beats 'Tutor.' The voice-profile GPT (lesson 14) that drafts everything in your words. The 'Skeptic' — 'challenge my plan, find the holes, no cheerleading' — secretly everyone's favorite. The rule: if you can't describe its job in one sentence, it will disappoint you. Every lesson in this course that you loved? It's a GPT waiting to be born. Mine was. That's the brand of this place, after all.

Sharing and maintaining

The underrated feature: share it. Give the link to your partner ('here, use the Family CFO'), your kid's other parent, your team. Your expertise, packaged. Inside the family or team, sharing is safe — check the sharing options so it stays private-by-default, and remember: files you upload go along for the ride, so share carefully (lesson 19 reflexes). Maintenance is light: when an answer disappoints, don't argue with the chat — go edit the instructions. That's the difference between a chat (argue) and an employee (update their training). Version one is allowed to be imperfect. Employees get better with feedback.

Copy-ready prompt

GPT instruction template: 'You are [name], who [one-sentence job]. Tone: [direct / warm / blunt]. When [situation], you [behavior]. Never [deal-breaker]. Start every conversation by [opening move]. If the user's request is unclear, [ask one question].'

Starter prompts to include: '[most common request]' / '[second most common]' / 'review the files and summarize what's changed since last time.'

Refinement loop: in the builder preview, run a real task. When the output disappoints, add one sentence to the instructions fixing exactly that. Repeat until it stops disappointing you.

💪 Exercise

Build one Custom GPT (or equivalent) today for a recurring job in your life: name, instructions from the template, one file, three starter prompts. Test it on a real task, refine once, and share it with one person.
