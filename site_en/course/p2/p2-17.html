A CV That Passes AI Screeners

🎯 What you'll be able to do: Write a CV that survives the automated filters and human skims standing between you and AI job interviews.
Alex · Play the lecturer

How screening actually works

Two filters run before the human: AI/ATS screening — software parses your CV and scores it against the job description for keywords, titles, skills, and experience signals — and the recruiter skim, roughly 7 seconds: name, current role, skills, last two jobs. Both fail on the same things: fancy layouts, text inside images, weird section names, missing keywords. So the winning format is boring on purpose: single column, standard sections (Summary, Experience, Projects, Skills, Education), normal fonts, .docx or clean PDF. Your design instincts are welcome at the interview; at screening, they're a liability. Boring is the new black.

The master CV and the tailoring pass

Keep a master CV with everything, then tailor a copy per application — 15 minutes, not a rewrite. The tailoring ritual:

1. Copy the exact language of the job description — if they say 'AI automation,' don't write 'workflow AI.' Screeners match strings.

2. Reorder skills so the listed ones come first.

3. Rewrite your summary line to mirror the role's title and mission.

4. Match titles where honest — 'Prompt Engineer' self-descriptions vary wildly; if your work matches 'AI Automation Specialist,' say that on this application.

Honest mirroring of vocabulary is not lying; it's translation. Lying is lying. Don't.

Bullets that carry evidence

The formula: Action + scope + result. Weak: 'Responsible for automating processes.' Strong: 'Built an n8n agent that triaged 200 support emails weekly, cutting first-response time 40%.' Numbers everywhere you can — rows processed, hours saved, approval rates, pass rates on your test logs. Your Module 4 projects are bullet goldmine: each one already *has* numbers (test pass rates, eval scores, time saved). List them under a Projects section with links — GitHub or live demos. In AI hiring of 2026, a linked, tested project outranks any course certificate, including ours — the certificate gets you past HR; the projects get you past the technical screen. You have both.

The AI-assisted CV workflow

Use AI without sounding like it:

1. Paste the job description + your master CV → ask for a match score and missing keywords.

2. Ask it to rewrite your 5 weakest bullets into action-scope-result form — then edit by hand until it sounds like you.

3. Run a hostile review pass: 'You are a skeptical recruiter with 7 seconds. What concerns you about this CV?'

4. Final human read-aloud — if a sentence doesn't survive being spoken, rewrite it.

Warning: recruiters now spot generic AI-CV smell instantly — the same voice, the same hollow phrases. Your voice samples from Lesson 15 are the antidote. AI drafts; you decide. That's the order, always.

Copy-ready prompt

Here is a job description and my current CV: [PASTE BOTH]. Give me: (1) keyword match score out of 100, (2) the 5 most important keywords I'm missing, (3) a rewritten summary line and my 3 weakest bullets improved using action-scope-result. Don't invent experience I don't have.

You are a skeptical hiring manager with 7 seconds to skim this CV: [PASTE CV]. List your first 3 impressions, your 3 biggest doubts, and the one change that would most improve interview chances.
