LinkedIn Profile Optimization

🎯 What you'll be able to do: Turn your LinkedIn profile into a recruiter magnet — keyword-searchable, credible, and clearly positioned for AI roles.
Alex · Play the lecturer

The headline: your search listing

The headline follows you into every search result, comment, and message — it's 220 characters of prime real estate, and 'Open to new opportunities' wastes every one. Formula: what you do + tools + value. Weak: 'Product Manager.' Strong: 'AI Automation Specialist | I build agents with n8n, Zapier & LLMs | Ex-PM — I turn messy processes into pipelines.' Recruiters search role keywords ('AI Automation Specialist', 'Prompt Engineer', 'AI Ops'), so your *target title* must appear verbatim — pick the title from Lesson 21's market map and wear it. Update it as you aim at different roles; it's a label, not a tattoo.

The about section: 20 seconds to convince

Recruiters who click give your About ~20 seconds. Structure it like a landing page:

  • Line 1: the hook — what you do and for whom. (The first line is all that shows before 'see more' — spend real time on it.)
  • Proof: 3-4 lines with your artifacts — 'Built a customer-support agent (live demo), a RAG document analyzer, and a data-cleaning pipeline with full audit logs.' Link them.
  • Tools & keywords: one natural-sounding paragraph naming your stack — n8n, Zapier, APIs, function calling, RAG, prompt engineering.
  • The ask: 'Open to AI automation, AI Ops, and prompt engineering roles — DM me.'

Write in first person, human voice, zero clichés ('passionate about leveraging synergies' is a crime). AI can draft it; your voice edits it. Lesson 15's voice file works here too.

Experience, skills, and the credibility layer

Experience: each role gets 2-4 bullets in the same action-scope-result format as your CV — including projects done in this course, which can live under a 'BINA Academy — AI Career Track' entry or freelance-style entries. Skills section: add everything true — AI automation, n8n, Zapier, prompt engineering, RAG, API integration — because recruiters filter by skill tags, and skills also feed LinkedIn's search index. Featured section: your gold shelf — links to live demos, your GitHub, your best post. A recruiter seeing three clickable, working projects in your Featured section is a recruiter half-convinced before any message. Verify every claim is real; you'll be tested on all of it in interviews.

Activity: proof of life

Searchability gets you found; activity gets you trusted. The sustainable minimum: one post a week — a lesson learned from a project, a small demo with a screenshot, a question to your network. Your Lesson 15 pipeline literally automates this. Comment thoughtfully on posts in the AI automation space — comments are networking at scale, visible to every reader of that thread. Turn on 'Open to Work' (recruiters-only mode if you prefer discretion). And clean the basics: real photo, real name, location set — profiles missing these drop in search ranking. Activity compounds: six months of weekly evidence is a public track record, which is the one thing a certificate can't fake.

Copy-ready prompt

Write 3 headline options for my LinkedIn. Target role: [TARGET TITLE]. My background: [1-2 LINES]. Stack: [TOOLS]. Each under 220 characters, specific, no clichés, human-sounding. Then tell me which one you'd pick as a recruiter and why.

Here is my draft About section: [PASTE IT], plus my voice samples: [PASTE 2 SAMPLES]. Rewrite it to: hook in line 1, proof with project links, natural keyword paragraph, clear ask. First person, no buzzwords, about 150 words.
